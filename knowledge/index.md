# Wazuh lab knowledge bundle

Runbooks, architecture decisions and reference notes for the read-only Wazuh
lab assistant. Served read-only by the `list_knowledge` / `read_knowledge`
tools of `wazuh_mcp`; answers cite the concept file.

# Subdirectories

* [runbooks](runbooks/index.md) - What to do when a typical lab alert or condition appears.
* [decisions](decisions/index.md) - Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) of the assistant.
* [reference](reference/index.md) - Lab environment and Wazuh severity levels.
